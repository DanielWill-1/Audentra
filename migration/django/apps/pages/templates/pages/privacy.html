Legal & data handling

Privacy Notice

Last updated October 17, 2026. This notice describes the current VoiceForm application. Hosting, provider account settings, and retention controls can change how a deployment handles data.

Voice and AI features use cloud processing

In the current configuration, VoiceForm sends recordings and form-filling conversation data to Groq after you opt in. Typed messages also use Groq. You can review the data flow below before using those features. Per-user provider selection and bring-your-own-key controls are not available in this release.

Before the first cloud AI request, you must accept a one-time consent prompt. That choice is stored in this browser for the current policy version, not as an account-level record. You can withdraw it from the voice form screen; the server then blocks future cloud AI requests until you opt in again.

What moves where

Your data flow

Recorded speech

After you opt in, the browser sends a recording to the VoiceForm server. The server forwards the audio to Groq Whisper Large V3 Turbo for transcription and returns the transcript. Accepted clips are limited to 7.5 MiB; clips above 2.5 MiB may be buffered to temporary disk by Django while being handled.

Chat and form draft

After you opt in, the server sends your message, recent conversation, form fields, and current draft values to Groq GPT-OSS 120B. The model response is checked against the form fields before the draft is updated. The app does not save chat history as a separate database record.

Spoken reply

When text-to-speech is used after you opt in, the assistant reply text is sent to Groq Orpheus and generated audio is returned to the browser. VoiceForm does not save that generated audio as a form record.

Saved data

What VoiceForm stores

Saved forms and templates

When you choose Save, completed form values and template details are stored in the configured Supabase database. Saved form records are scoped to their owner by row-level access policies. They remain until deleted in the app; this code does not set an automatic expiry for them. Database backups and provider retention are separate.

Account and session

Supabase Auth handles account identity. The Django application keeps a server-side session record in its configured database; its absolute lifetime is limited to 14 days. The app uses essential session and CSRF cookies. In production settings, the session cookie is HttpOnly and both cookies are Secure.

Template file uploads are kept in the private template-files Supabase Storage bucket and are available according to the template owner and sharing permissions. Deleting a saved record or file does not establish when provider backups or other provider-held copies expire. The current app does not provide self-service account deletion.

Third-party processing

Groq data retention

The app owner confirms Zero Data Retention (ZDR) is disabled for the Groq organization used by this app. Groq says inference inputs and outputs are not retained by default, but may be temporarily logged for reliability troubleshooting or abuse investigations for up to 30 days (or longer if legally required). Groq also collects usage metadata that does not include customer prompts or outputs. Groq says retained customer data is stored in United States GCP buckets.

Read Groq’s data controls documentation

Logs, cookies, and deployment

The Django application’s logging filter replaces application log messages with fixed event labels and removes exception details before writing them. This does not cover logs collected by a hosting provider, reverse proxy, database provider, or Groq. The production Django settings require HTTPS redirection and secure cookies; the public hosting region, infrastructure logs, and backup retention depend on the operator’s deployment.

Your choices

  • Cloud chat and speech are blocked until you opt in. Typing instead of recording still sends the typed message to Groq after you opt in.
  • You can withdraw cloud AI consent from the voice form screen. Future chat and speech requests are blocked until you opt in again.
  • You can review the completed draft before saving it.
  • You can delete individual saved form records in the Filled Forms area.
  • Do not enter regulated or highly sensitive information unless your organization has reviewed this deployment and its provider terms.

For privacy questions, use the Contact page. This notice does not claim HIPAA/HITECH, GDPR, CCPA, SOC 2, or ISO certification or compliance.
