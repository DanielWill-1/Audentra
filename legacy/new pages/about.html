The Problem

Professionals spend up to 40% of their day manually feeding databases.

In clinical medicine, physical infrastructure engineering, aviation diagnostics, and field inspection, domain specialists are hired for their critical discernment—not their keyboard typing speed.

Yet regulatory overhead, ERP rigidity, and clinical EHR demands consume hours of every shift. Natural human speech operates at roughly 140 to 170 words per minute; average typing struggles at 40 words per minute. The cognitive tax of translating observations through tiny dropdowns and modal forms creates a compounding administrative deficit.

“Traditional transcription just replaces a blank form with a monolithic wall of text that still requires manual copy-pasting, reading, and auditing.”

— Audentra Design Rationale, Whitepaper v1

Philosophical Stance

“Speech shouldn’t merely become text. It should become useful, structured information.”

We prioritize technical guarantees over generative hallucination. Audentra operates on three non-negotiable architectural dogmas:

01 / DETERMINISTIC SCHEMAS

Strict Contract Alignment

Freeform generative responses are prohibited from downstream persistence. Every vocal utterance is mapped through rigid JSONSchema, Pydantic, or Zod schemas with compile-time validation. Missing values trigger explicit human verification flags rather than silent guesses.

  • Zero schema hallucinations
  • Coerced enum types & timestamps
02 / AUDITABILITY

Confidence & Origin Tracing

Every field populated in the output payload includes character-level millisecond audio timestamps and acoustic certainty scores. Operators and auditors can click any extracted value to instantly hear the exact four seconds of spoken context that generated it.

  • Sub-second audio token mapping
  • Human-in-the-loop fallback review
03 / CONTROL

Operator-Owned Governance

The software answers solely to the practitioner deploying it. No hidden system prompts, no unexpected model updates that alter extraction precision, and no remote telemetry pingbacks reporting domain activity back to a centralized vendor server.

  • Air-gapped deployment capable
  • Portable configuration via YAML/JSON
Data Sovereignty

High-stakes industries cannot risk opaque black-box clouds.

When audio contains confidential clinician-patient dialogues, proprietary industrial patent findings, or sensitive human resource appraisals, shipping raw audio to third-party proprietary inference APIs poses unacceptable compliance, liability, and regulatory vulnerabilities.

Audentra is licensed under the permissive Apache 2.0 License. You can inspect every line of parser logic, verify network isolation inside your own Kubernetes clusters, compile binaries locally, and modify the underlying extractors without vendor consent or recurring seat taxes.

Engineering Specs

Architectural Direction

A composable pipeline separating audio streaming, acoustic tokenization, and schema coercion into cleanly decoupled layers.

TARGET: LINUX · DARWIN · WASM
devices
FRONTEND INTERFACE

TypeScript & WebAudio

Zero-dependency client audio streaming. Uses low-latency AudioWorklets for real-time ring buffering, localized VAD (voice activity detection), and immediate visual feedback.

Latency: < 25ms buffer
memory
CORE ENGINE

Python & Rust Pipeline

Async message passing with Rust-based streaming tokenizers. Manages memory safety, concurrency, and high-throughput multi-speaker channel ingestion without overhead.

Async Tokio + PyO3 bindings

Whisper.cpp & Self-Hosted
